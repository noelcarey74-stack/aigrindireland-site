Account and privacy

Delete your AI Grind Ireland account

You can permanently delete your account and its study data.

Delete in the app

Sign in to AI Grind Ireland, select Delete my account in your account controls, then follow the on-screen confirmation. This removes your student account and associated learning data, including saved grinds, answers and progress. This cannot be undone.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access your account, email aigrindireland@gmail.com from your registered email and request deletion. We may need to verify ownership before proceeding.

Subscriptions are separate

Deleting an AI Grind Ireland account does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately in the store where you subscribed. Certain transaction records or limited service/security logs may be retained by payment processors or service providers where required by law.
