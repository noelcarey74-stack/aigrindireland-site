A structured study flow

How an AI Grind works.

AGI is designed to keep study focused: select the correct curriculum, work through one topic, get feedback, then use the result to guide revision.

Inside AI Grind Ireland

A structured grind, not a blank chat

Choose a curriculum topic, work through teaching and questions step by step, get feedback, and carry the result into progress and revision.

1. Make AGI yours

On setup, the student chooses a school year, programme, subjects and levels, plus a preferred explanation style: Simple, Balanced or Detailed. AGI uses that setup to select the relevant Irish curriculum version.

2. Choose what you want to do

Learn

Build understanding of a curriculum topic step by step.

Practice

Use saved progress and recent answers to focus on weaker areas.

Ask AGI

Explain exactly what is confusing you, within the subject and level you selected.

Exam

Work through original exam-style questions with marks and teaching feedback.

3. Answer one step at a time

A grind is a multi-step session. AGI presents a question or teaching step, the student responds, and the app provides feedback before moving through the session. The Voice Tutor can read questions and feedback aloud when requested.

4. Turn results into revision

Completed work updates topic mastery, attempts, recent scores and the next review timing. When a topic becomes due for revision, AGI can surface that on the home/progress experience.

Official sources included: AGI gives students quick access to curriculum and exam-reference links whenever they want to check the source material.
