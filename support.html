Help & support

AI Grind Ireland Support

Help with accounts, study setup, AGI Premium, purchases, AI permissions and app access.

Contact support

Email aigrindireland@gmail.com and include “AI Grind Ireland” in the subject line.

Please do not include passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

Subscription help

Purchases are handled by Apple. Use Restore Purchases in the app if you have already subscribed. To change or cancel a subscription, open your Apple Account subscription settings.

Account help

You can edit your study setup, change AI study permissions, sign out and permanently delete your account from the Account section of the app.

Study and exam information

AGI includes direct official-source links for curriculum and examination reference. Students can open these from the Sources area whenever they need them.

Safety

AGI is a study tool, not an emergency, medical or counselling service. If you or someone else is unsafe, contact a trusted adult or an appropriate emergency or support service.
