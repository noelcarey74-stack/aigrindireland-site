Try the Junior Cycle algebra challenge

Before watching the answer, solve 3x + 5 = 20. First subtract 5 from both sides: 3x = 15. Divide by 3 and x = 5. Check your solution: 3 × 5 + 5 = 20. The important revision habit is to write each operation, not just guess the answer.

Practise a topic, not just a question

A short maths challenge is a useful starting point, but revision works better when you repeat the method with different numbers and explain each step. AI Grind Ireland helps Junior Cycle and Leaving Cert students choose subjects, ask for help, practise topics and keep track of revision.

Build a study routine

Choose one curriculum topic, work through an explanation, answer a few questions and return to weaker areas another day. This page offers general study tips; always use your teacher's guidance and official examination materials alongside any AI study tool.
