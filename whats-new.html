Version 1.1 highlights

Less friction. More focus on learning.

AGI is designed to help you understand a topic, practise questions and see where you need more revision — not just produce an answer on demand.

  • Refreshed design: a clearer layout for study and revision.
  • Watch AGI in action: a short tour before sign-up, also available from Home and Account.
  • Easier account access: improved sign-in, registration and password recovery.
  • Clearer Premium: subscription, trial and Restore Purchases messages are easier to follow.
  • Readability and stability: general improvements throughout the app.
