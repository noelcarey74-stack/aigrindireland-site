Revision that remembers the work

Topic mastery, recent scores and review timing help students see what is improving and what should come back into focus next.

Built around Senior Cycle programmes

AGI supports Leaving Certificate Established, Leaving Certificate Vocational Programme, Leaving Certificate Applied and Senior Cycle Level 1/Level 2 pathways. The app filters study choices to match the student’s selected programme.

Exam-style practice without pretending to be an official paper

Exam Practice creates original exam-style questions with marks and teaching feedback. AGI also includes an Official Sources area so students can reach relevant official curriculum, exam-paper and marking-scheme sources when they need authoritative information.

Revision that remembers previous work

AGI records topic mastery, attempts and recent grind results. Practice can use that saved progress, and revision timing helps bring weaker or due topics back into focus.
